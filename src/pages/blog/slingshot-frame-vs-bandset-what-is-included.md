A wooden slingshot frame is the handheld body. A complete flat bandset generally combines the working bands and pouch. Tying tape is separate binding material used with suitable attachment systems. Product photographs may show these items together, so confirm the written contents and quantity before paying rather than assuming every pictured accessory is included.

It is an easy misunderstanding: a frame sits on a display stand, bands appear in another photograph, and several rolls of tape share one image. The gallery makes sense visually, but the order still needs a clear list of contents.

At Zenith Fork Studio, orders are confirmed by email. That is a useful moment to resolve exactly which piece, supplies, and quantities you want.

## The frame is the wooden body

The frame includes the handle and forks. Some designs have fitted tip hardware; others have traditional wooden attachment surfaces. Those differences should be described in the listing, but they do not automatically establish whether a bandset is supplied.

For example, the [85 mm sculpted solid-wood frame](/products/fork-sculpted-solid-wood-85/) is listed at USD 169, with 18 mm fork tips. Its description asks buyers to confirm attachment details and included accessories by email. That is more precise than assuming a ready-to-use setup from a photograph.

A stand used to support a frame in the gallery is a photography aid unless it is specifically included in the confirmed sale. The same principle applies to any background tool or packaging shown for scale.

## A bandset is a different product

The working bands connect the frame to the pouch. A finished replacement bandset usually includes both latex strips and the attached pouch, but sellers can use the term differently. Read the actual contents.

Our [Pro Tapered Flat Bandsets](/products/bands-pro-latex-pack-10/) are listed as a 10-pack with 20–12 mm tapered latex and microfiber pouches. They are separate from a wooden frame and from the narrow tying material used at an attachment point.

Confirm suitable active length and attachment compatibility before fitting a replacement set. Having a compatible-looking pouch and latex does not establish that the setup is appropriate for your draw length.

If that terminology is new, our [draw-length measurement guide](/blog/how-to-measure-slingshot-draw-length/) explains the difference between user draw length and working band length.

## Tying tape does not replace the working bands

The [20 m amber tying tape roll](/products/bands-amber-tying-tape-20m/) supplies narrow binding material. Its purpose is to secure bands using an appropriate tying method; it is not a spool of ready-made shooting bandsets.

The listed price is USD 12.80 for one 20 m roll. Several rolls appear in some photographs to show the packaging and the unwound strip. Those photographs do not change the sale quantity.

Likewise, a metre count describes the amount of material supplied. It is not a guaranteed number of completed attachments, since the length used varies with the frame and wrapping method. Our [amber tying tape guide](/blog/amber-tying-tape-for-slingshot-bands/) covers those details.

## Hardware belongs to a specific design

Screw-down caps, tip plates, and traditional tying surfaces are not interchangeable descriptions. A brass fitting visible in a thumbnail may need a closer look before you know how the band is secured.

When buying a frame with fitted hardware, ask whether any necessary tool is supplied and how replacement parts are handled. If the listing does not say, do not assume. When buying a traditional frame, confirm what tying material is needed and whether it is included.

The [quick-clamp versus wrap-and-tuck article](/blog/quick-clamp-vs-wrap-and-tuck-slingshot-bands/) can help you identify the questions to ask. It is not a substitute for the instructions for the particular piece.

## Confirm the unit as well as the item

“One” can mean one frame, one complete bandset, one pack, or one roll. Use the full product name and unit in your inquiry so neither side has to infer the quantity.

For example:

> I would like the 85 mm sculpted wooden frame and one 20 m roll of amber tying tape. Please confirm whether any bandset is included with the frame, what additional supplies are needed, and the final order contents before payment.

If you want spare bandsets, name the pack separately. Add your delivery country and region so availability and shipping can be checked together with the contents.

## Keep the final confirmation specific

Before paying, the email confirmation should identify the exact wooden piece, accessories, quantities, final amount, and shipping arrangements. Keep that message so you can compare the delivered contents with what was agreed.

For a one-of-one frame, include the product name or link. Pattern names may repeat across different pieces, but grain and hand-shaped details do not. A product link reduces the chance of discussing one frame while ordering another.

After delivery, use the [arrival inspection guide](/blog/check-handmade-wooden-slingshot-on-arrival/) to check the item and supplied accessories. If anything is unclear, ask before assembling or using the setup. Accurate contents make the purchase easier to understand and the first inspection much simpler.
