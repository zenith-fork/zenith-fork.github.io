## Golden grain is an appearance description

The name describes the warm golden-brown colour and darker flowing lines visible in the photographs. It is not a botanical wood identification. This piece is confirmed as solid wood, but its species has not been confirmed.

That distinction matters when comparing it with our [90 mm Desert Ironwood frame](/products/fork-desert-ironwood-classic-90/). Similar warm colours do not make the two woods identical. Choose this frame for its documented construction, shape, and pictured appearance rather than assigning a species from colour alone.

Expect some change in brightness and warmth between your screen and ordinary daylight. Follow distinctive grain lines across the gallery to understand the actual piece. The front, handheld view, side profile, reverse, and grip photograph each add something different.

## What the 15 mm tips tell you

The 15 mm tip measurement describes their width. The photos also show metal plates at the tops. Neither fact, by itself, establishes the correct installation procedure for a bandset.

Ask for the intended attachment method and any needed tying material or tools before paying. Do not assume that a metal plate functions like the screw-down quick clamps on another model.

Replacement bands are a separate decision. Latex dimensions and active length need to suit the complete setup and your draw length.
