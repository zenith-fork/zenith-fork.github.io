## A broader frame without extra height

Outside fork width and overall height are separate design choices. This piece is wider across the tips than our 80 mm frames, but at 125 mm high it sits between the 120 mm Flying Tiger round-tip and the approximately 130 mm Golden-Grain frame.

That combination is worth considering if you like a broad upper outline without choosing the tallest handle in the collection. It is not a large-hand sizing guarantee. The place where your fingers settle still depends on handle depth, waist shape, and the hold you use.

The 20 mm tips also deserve their own line in the specification. They describe the geometry at the top of the frame, not a recommendation to fit a particular band width or heavier setup. Confirm the intended attachment method before ordering bands for it.

## Why the side and reverse views matter

The front view shows the U-shaped outline. The reverse and angled views explain how the handle rounds away from that outline and how the grain continues across the carved surfaces.
